Before you buy

The questions worth answering straight.

What exactly do I get?

PDF files, downloaded immediately after checkout. No app, no login, no shipping, no subscription and no upsells afterward — you pay once. Read them on a phone, a tablet, a laptop, or print the pages you want in the kitchen.

Can I print these at home? Will the pages fill 8.5×11 paper?

Both books are laid out at 6×9 — a standard book trim, not a full letter page — so on an 8.5×11 sheet the page sits with a border of white around it rather than running edge to edge. Print at "actual size" and that border becomes a deliberate margin: room to three-hole-punch for a binder, jot a note or an ingredient swap next to a recipe, or just give the page somewhere to be handled without smudging the text. If you want the page as large as the sheet allows instead, choose "fit to printable area" in the print dialog; it will still leave a thin margin on two sides, since 6×9 and 8.5×11 are not quite the same shape.

Is the canning information actually safe?

Every processing time, pressure and headspace figure in the Manual is cross-checked against the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning and the National Center for Home Food Preservation. Where a popular method is not tested, the book says so plainly instead of repeating it.

Do I need special equipment?

For the Manual, a water bath canner covers most of it; a pressure canner is required for low-acid food and the book explains exactly why. The Cookbook needs nothing but a pot, a skillet and, for one part of it, a slow cooker.

I do not can anything. Is the Cookbook still for me?

Yes, and that is deliberate. Every ingredient list gives the home-canned amount and the supermarket equivalent side by side. If you have never sealed a jar in your life, every recipe still works from cans off the shelf.

Have the recipes been tested?

They are cross-checked for method and for food safety, and they have not each been individually kitchen-tested. That is stated inside the book too. Cooking times and seasoning are starting points — your cooker and your taste will move them.

Is this a survival or prepper book?

The Manual covers long-term storage honestly, including a three-month pantry. But neither book is written for a collapse. This is food you eat in an ordinary week, which is the only reason a pantry is ever worth keeping.

Can I buy one now and the other later?

Yes. They are sold separately for that reason. The bundle simply saves $15 if you already know you want both.

Reply to your receipt within 7 days and you will get a refund. No form to fill in and no reason required. It is a short window because these are digital files — once a PDF is downloaded it cannot be handed back — so have a look soon after you buy.
